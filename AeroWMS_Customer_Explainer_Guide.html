1. What is AeroWMS & Why Do Businesses Need It?

Traditional warehouses rely on spreadsheets, paper registers, or basic accounting software that only show total stock numbers. They cannot answer the two most vital operational questions: "Which exact shelf is this product on?" and "Which batch must we ship out first to avoid expiry?"

AeroWMS bridges the gap between physical warehouse operations and executive decision-making. It operates as an end-to-end digital nervous system across multiple branches, from supplier intake down to the customer dispatch door.

4. Core Modules & Capabilities Breakdown

📦 1. Inbound Stock Receiving

Record vendor deliveries, generate or scan barcodes, capture batch numbers, manufacturing & expiry dates, and assign target bins immediately upon arrival.

🚀 2. Outbound Order Fulfillment (FEFO)

When fulfilling orders, AeroWMS automatically prioritizes batches expiring earliest, preventing old stock from turning into dead stock and protecting profitability.

🔄 3. Multi-Branch Transfers

Seamless movement between central hubs and retail branches. Stock deducted from the source warehouse is tracked in-transit until acknowledged and binned at destination.

⚖️ 4. Stock Adjustments & Cycle Counts

Built-in maker-checker approval workflows for cycle count discrepancies, damaged packages, or customer returns. Preserves integrity without unapproved edits.

📷 5. Optical Barcode & QR Scanner

Integrated live camera viewfinder powered by `html5-qrcode` with an animated laser guide. Also supports traditional USB / Bluetooth hardware laser scanners.

📊 6. Executive BI & Reporting

Real-time metrics: Out-of-Stock count, Reorder Level warnings, 7-Day In/Out velocity chart, and one-click export to PDF Audit Reports & Excel Ledgers.

7. Client FAQs & Handling Customer Objections

Customer Question Proven Answer
"Do we need to buy expensive barcode guns?" No. AeroWMS includes built-in optical camera recognition. Any smartphone, tablet, or laptop camera functions as a scanner. If you already have USB/Bluetooth laser scanners, they work seamlessly out of the box.
"Can we handle multiple branches in different cities?" Yes. The system has full multi-branch support. You can track stock per branch and initiate formal Inter-Branch Transfers with in-transit tracking.
"How does it prevent expired goods from being sold?" AeroWMS enforces FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out). When creating dispatch orders, the system automatically selects and suggests the batch expiring earliest, plus flags items 30 days before expiration on the dashboard.
"Is our corporate data safe and secure?" Yes. AeroWMS uses enterprise-grade multi-tenant data isolation, Spring Security 6 with stateless JWT tokens, and role-based permissions (Admin, Branch Manager, Staff, Viewer).
"Can we export our inventory for accounting audits?" Yes. With a single click, download formatted PDF Inventory Balance Reports (A4 landscape) or Excel (.xlsx) Stock Ledgers covering all historical transactions.
